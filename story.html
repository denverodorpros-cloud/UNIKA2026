Brand Story

Outdoor gear with a one-tree pledge

We’re two outdoor enthusiasts based in Denver, Colorado. We started UNIKA to make practical gear for days outside, even when the forecast doesn’t cooperate.

Mission

Our pledge is to plant one tree for every product we sell. We’ve worked with One Tree Planted to support reforestation.
